Tip of the Day

PSA: Microsoft 365 is way more powerful than you're using it 🚀

Copilot · Meetings

Never miss an action item again

Copilot recaps every meeting with a transcript, summary, and a clean list of action items — even if you joined late or "accidentally" went on mute for 20 minutes. Find it in the meeting chat after the call.

Copilot · Chat & Channels

TL;DR on a 300-message thread

Open any chat or channel and ask Copilot to "summarize the last 48 hours" or "what decisions were made?" It reads the whole thread so you don't have to. No more doom-scrolling for context.

Copilot · Outlook

Draft email replies using your meeting notes

Copilot in Outlook can pull context straight from your Teams meetings and chats when drafting a reply. Mention "use last week's call notes" and it fills in the relevant details — no copy-paste needed.

Viva Insights

Let AI defend your focus time

Viva Insights (via the Teams sidebar) automatically books daily focus blocks and silences notifications during them. It also shows you how much of your week is eaten by meetings — sometimes that number is a wake-up call.

Copilot · SharePoint

Find any file with a plain English question

In the Teams search bar, Copilot can surface files, emails, and meeting recordings by meaning, not just keywords. Ask "find the Q3 budget deck Sara shared in July" and it actually works. It's your personal M365 brain.

Copilot Studio

Build a custom team bot — no code required

Copilot Studio lets you create a custom AI assistant for your team in under an hour. Connect it to your SharePoint docs, internal FAQs, or ticketing system. Drop it into a Teams channel and watch your teammates become self-sufficient.
